## Founder and builder · [Mainthread](https://mainthread.ai)

A builder and entrepreneur at heart. I love technology because I can build systems that meaningfully improve people's lives, bring people closer together, and help them achieve their goals. I recognize friction as a signal of what's misaligned, and I learn how to fix it by building: each version reveals a better arrangement of what's possible.

It started in high school, building websites and selling web hosting and domain names. For the last ten years I've built systems for clients, advised them, and grown those relationships, and I've founded two companies of my own. Today I run Mainthread, my research-led engineering and design studio, where I build agents, MCP servers, knowledge graphs, dev tooling, and web, mobile, and desktop apps, and research how teams of AI agents work best.
